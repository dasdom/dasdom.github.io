Only few people (if any) can work 8 hours or more per day for 5 days a week and be productive, healthy and a social being over a extended period of time.
But still this is the norm and expected.
12 years ago I realized that I can only be productive and healthy when working 32 hours per week.
So I switched to "part time".
[But science suggests](https://theweek.com/articles/696644/why-should-work-4-hours-day-according-science) I'm still wasting about 12 hours each week.

"Part time" means less salary.
I'm punished for keeping me healthy and productive.
I could easily "work" 40 hours per week by jeopardizing my productivity, my health and my social life.
I choose not to do that.

With only 8 more hours free time I [wrote several books](https://dasdom.dev/books/), spoke at conferences and [published some
apps](https://apps.apple.com/de/developer/dominik-hauser/id325836858).
I'm spending more time with my children and I'm trying to be a better partner to my wife.

What could I do with 12 more hours for myself and my family?
What kind of a dad could I be with so much time?
How would our society look like if more people would only work the hours they are productive?
I guess we would be happier and [probably way worse customers](https://www.businessinsider.com/the-real-reason-for-the-40-hour-workweek-2014-6).

How about you? 
How many of your work hours per day are actually needed for your output?
